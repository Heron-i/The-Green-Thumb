# Problem memo -- The Green Thumb (Lily Heeren, Lucas Jenkins)

## The user
Our device can be used by any person who is a household plant owner and would like to monitor their plants for better care of them. When it comes to the place, our device can be used in any household that holds plants and would like to monitor them more deeply.

## The problem
The problem we are trying to resolve with our device is incorrect care of plants. Household plants can be quite expensive and time consuming. While most people might think plants are easy to keep alive, a lot of times they can be over-watered, get the incorrect amount of sun, or overall not live as long as they could because of how they are cared for. These problems can happen a lot and the main cost is monetary, time, and the life of the plants themselves.

## Why a device
This device is a good idea because we would be able to monitor and report about a plant at all times, this would allow the user to not constantly monitor their plant and overall give their plants better lifespans. A phone app can not reasonably solve the problem we have and the hardware is essential so we can monitor the stats of the plants directly.

## The sensors
The sensors we plan to use in our project are light and moisture sensors. The light sensors will allow us to monitor how much sunlight the plant is receiving and the moisture sensor will allow us to monitor when the plant will need water, or if it is being over-wattered. We also may decide to use temperature sensors to read what temperature the plant and surrounding area is for optimal plant life. These cooperate with our device as they are essential to monitoring the health and needs of the plant.

Our software heavily relies on the data we gather from our device, so if one piece of the data or software is slightly off then we could have massive problems. Overall, our most likely sink is poor execution of software with the data collected.
